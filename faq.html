Patient information is automatically de-identified before scan data leaves the facility. supports a HIPAA-aligned workflow designed for VA and DoD healthcare environments.

Prosthetics & Workflow
After the patient's scan is completed and de-identified, the scan data and provider prescription are transmitted for fabrication. Custom orthotics are manufactured to the provider's specifications using the digital scan and prescribed accommodations.
3D Orthotics coordinates patient follow-up, delivery verification, and support throughout the process. Our team works directly with the Veteran on delivery, fit verification, and any questions about their orthotics, reducing the administrative burden on clinic staff and Prosthetics departments.
If an adjustment is needed, the 3D Orthotics team coordinates directly with the patient, provider, and manufacturing team to evaluate the concern and arrange the appropriate modification. The goal is to resolve fit and comfort issues quickly while keeping the administrative load off providers and Prosthetics departments.

Equipment & Device Management
Although uses a de-identified workflow, equipment lifecycle management still matters. When the iPad is no longer needed, requires replacement, or is upgraded, the facility retains possession and control of the device. Before retirement or replacement, 3D Orthotics works with the facility to remove application data, terminate system access, and support the device transition in accordance with applicable VA facility requirements and local procedures. The goal is a smooth transition that protects patient privacy, data security, and program continuity.
During onboarding, the facility's Logistics or Equipment Management team may assign a barcode, asset tag, or inventory record to the iPad in accordance with local procedures. When the iPad requires replacement, upgrade, retirement, or removal from service, the facility retains possession and control of the device. 3D Orthotics works directly with the facility to remove application data, terminate system access, and support the device transition per applicable facility requirements and local procedures.
