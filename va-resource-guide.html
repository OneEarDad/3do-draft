Facility Onboarding & Consult Process

This walks VA podiatrists, Prosthetics departments, and clinical staff through the consult process for . Because patient information is de-identified during scanning, each facility can establish a Prosthetics consult workflow that ties the patient consult to the appropriate DPID and ordering requirements.

During onboarding, 3D Orthotics works directly with Prosthetics and clinical staff to establish the consult template, DPID workflow, and ordering process for your facility. To get started, email us from your VA.gov email address with your facility name, station number, department, and role.
